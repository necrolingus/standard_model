Core Conceptual Distinction: Elementary vs. Composite
Fundamental Particles (Quarks, Leptons, and Bosons) are point-like and have no internal components. For example, a photon is an elementary gauge boson, not made of quarks. In contrast, Hadrons (such as protons and neutrons) are composite particles assembled from bound quarks held together by the strong force.
